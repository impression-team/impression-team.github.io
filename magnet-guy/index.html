About the game

Control magnet, guide character, navigate obstacles!

Welcome to the captivating world of Magnet Guy!

In this unique puzzle game, your task is to skillfully control the magnet by tapping the screen, magnetizing the character, and guiding them through intricate mazes. However, be cautious, as you'll need to avoid various obstacles to reach the exit!

Immerse yourself in the thrilling gameplay and solve all the puzzles in the Magnet Guy!
