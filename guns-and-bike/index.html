About the game

Hit the gas, jump ramps and blast gangsters in slow motion. Unlock new guns at the shop truck and take down tank bosses in explosive biker action!

Hit the gas and turn the streets into a slow-motion shootout.

In Guns and Bike, your motorcycle rides by itself – your job is to stay alive. Launch off ramps, drift through tight turns and slide under barriers while gangsters open fire from every side. When time slows down, line up your laser sight and take them out one by one.

  • Cinematic slow-motion gunfights on a speeding bike
  • Stunts: ramp jumps, 180° and 360° drifts, slides under obstacles
  • Pistol, rifle, SMG, machine gun, assault rifle and rocket launcher
  • Mid-ride shop truck: switch to a stronger weapon without stopping
  • Gangsters, police and tank boss fights
  • Checkpoints keep your progress
  • Simple one-finger controls

Ride fast. Aim true. Leave nothing standing.
