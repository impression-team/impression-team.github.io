// Who We Are

Independent Studio.
Infinite Imagination.

Impression Team Studio is an indie game development studio headquartered in Belgrade, Serbia. We design and build engaging games for mobile platforms, PC via Steam, and consoles — reaching players across the globe.

Our team combines technical expertise with a passion for creative gameplay, delivering titles that are easy to pick up and hard to put down. From idle tycoons to fast-paced action and satisfying puzzles, we build across a wide spectrum of genres.
