// The game

About the game

144 levels in 9 cozy chapters: slide tiles along their arrows, match three and collect a postcard album, from a sunrise dacha to a snowy hill.

Arrow Mahjong is a cozy puzzle where mahjong meets arrow tiles.

Every tile has an arrow. Tap a tile and it slides along its arrow into the tray, as long as the path to the edge of the board is clear. Three matching tiles in the tray disappear. Think about the order: if the tray fills up with tiles that don't match, the level is lost.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap a tile to send it along its arrow.
  • A tile leaves only when nothing blocks its way to the edge.
  • Collect three matching tiles in the 7-slot tray to clear them.
  • Clear the board and the tray to complete the level.

WHAT'S INSIDE

  • 144 levels in 9 themed chapters: a dacha at sunrise, a bakery, a country fair, a forest edge, evening tea, a lighthouse bay, a riverside picnic, a cozy room and a snowy hill.
  • Every level opens a piece of the chapter picture. Finished pictures go to your postcard album.
  • A gentle difficulty curve with a short tutorial. Levels marked with a star are a real challenge.
  • Hints when you need them: Undo, +1 Slot and Shuffle.
  • Large, clear tiles, a calm pace and no timer.
  • Sound and vibration can be turned off in settings.

Arrow Mahjong is free to play. It shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases.
