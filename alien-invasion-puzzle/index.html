About the game

Destroy scientists using tentacle mastery.

In the game Alien Invasion Puzzle you play as an alien spider. Breaking free from your cell, you use your tentacles to drag scientists through mazes, bypassing obstacles and puzzles. The player's task is to skillfully maneuver the tentacles, bring the scientists to your jaws, and avoid dangers. Strategy and logic will help you overcome challenges on each level of Alien Invasion Puzzle creating an exciting blend of puzzles and adventure.
