Mech Merge: Tower Defense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026.

Short version: Mech Merge has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress lives on your device. The only data that ever leaves it is what the app store you installed from (Apple App Store or Google Play) and Unity need to deliver a purchase you made.

Who we are

Mech Merge: Tower Defense is developed by Sergey Bragilevskiy. Contact for anything at all — a purchase that did not arrive, lost progress, a bug, or a privacy question: sergporke@gmail.com. We normally reply within a few working days.

What stays on your device

* Game progress: cores, shards, unlocked frames and PRIME frames, campaign progress, daily and weekly missions, best times, settings and tutorial state.

* Anonymous gameplay counters (for example "sector 2 started", "tutorial finished") that help us find bugs.

All of this is stored in the app's own local storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

Only in-app purchases send anything, and only when you make one:

* Apple (App Store), if you installed the game on iPhone or iPad. Apple processes the payment and tells the game whether the purchase succeeded. We never see your name, card or Apple Account details. Apple's own privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

* Google Play Billing, if you installed the game from Google Play on Android. Google processes the payment and tells the game whether the purchase succeeded. We never see your name, card or Google Account details. Google's own privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

* Unity In-App Purchasing. The game uses Unity's purchasing SDK to deliver purchases and to restore them on a new device. In the course of that, Unity may receive a device identifier and the fact that a purchase happened. This data is used only to make purchases work. It is not linked to your identity, it is not used for advertising, and it is not used for tracking. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

This matches the App Store privacy label and the Google Play Data safety section for this app: Device ID and Purchase History, collected for App Functionality, not linked to you, not shared, not used for tracking, encrypted in transit.

What the game never does

* No account and no login. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or address.

* No advertising, no advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), no third-party ad SDKs.

* No third-party analytics SDKs.

* No access to location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

* No chat, no user-generated content, no social features.

* We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

Purchases and restoring them

In-app purchases are optional. Nothing in the campaign requires payment. Permanent purchases (Command Pass, commanders, PRIME frames) can be restored on a new device with Settings → RESTORE PURCHASES, which asks Apple or Google Play (and Unity) which purchases belong to your Apple Account or Google Account.

Refunds are handled by the store you paid through. Apple: https://support.apple.com/billing. Google Play: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

Children

The game is rated 12+ on the App Store (Google Play shows the rating assigned for your region) and contains cartoon/fantasy violence (robots fighting insects). It has no chat, no user-generated content and no targeted advertising. It contains randomised supply crates, which are disclosed on the store page. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. Everything the game knows about you is on your device and disappears with the app. Purchase records held by Apple or Google are covered by their privacy policies; you can ask Apple or Google about them directly.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online features, accounts or analytics, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the in-game link in Settings will point to the new text.
