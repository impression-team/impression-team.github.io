About the game

You are a wildlife hero rescuing animals on the farmers' territory.

In "Dream Dart: Rescuing the Wild" you are a wildlife hero rescuing animals on the farmers' territory. Use a rifle with tranquilizer darts and a thermal scope. Detect and tranquilize animals, transport them to a safe distance, and set them free. Overcome levels with different animals and challenges. Become a nature defender in "Dream Dart: Rescuing the Wild"!

  • Unique gameplay: Play as a wildlife savior, using a rifle that shoots tranquilizer darts.
  • Rescue mission: Roam the village and help wild animals that have wandered onto the farmers' territory.
  • Thermal scope: Utilize a thermal scope to detect and aim at the animals.
  • Release into freedom: Tranquilize animals, transport them away from the village, and release them into the wild.
  • Quick reflexes and precision: Act swiftly to save as many animals as possible.
  • Diverse levels: Experience various types of wildlife, challenging routes, and trials.
