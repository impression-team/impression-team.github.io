Hero Hotel: Idle Tower Tycoon

Build, upgrade and grow rich

iPhone · iPad Android
// The game

About the game

Become a hero and build your hotel tower floor by floor! VIP guests fly in by helicopter, and new rewards wait for you every day.

Grab a brick and start building! In Hero Hotel you raise your own hotel tower floor by floor, welcome guests from the city bus and turn a tiny construction site into a booming hotel empire.

BUILD YOUR TOWER

Carry bricks, lay walls and finish one floor after another. Every new floor brings new rooms, more guests and more income. Buy elevators so guests and workers reach the top faster.

BECOME A HERO

Visit the Hero HQ and transform! Run up the hotel wall as Bolt, fly straight to the new floor as Aero, carry double loads as Shade or turn into Titan, a giant who carries 36 bricks at once.

HIRE HEROES

Hire hero helpers to build next to you and finish floors in record time. A free hero boost waits for you every day.

VIP GUESTS

Finish the hotel and open the helipad on the roof: VIP guests fly in by helicopter and pay five times the rent.

UPGRADE EVERY ROOM

Beds, TVs, tables, paintings, windows, floors and more: each upgrade makes a room more comfortable and raises the rent your guests pay.

OPEN A CAFE AND A SHOP

Hungry guests love a good cafe, and shoppers never leave empty-handed. Open both, speed up service and serve more customers at once.

COOL SKINS WITH BONUSES

Try on outfits in the 3D fitting room and pick your style. Many skins give a bonus: more speed, more bricks or more income. A new skin is on sale every day.

DAILY REWARDS AND CONTRACTS

Come back every day for growing rewards, complete three daily contracts, spin the lucky wheel and collect achievements.

IDLE INCOME

Your hotel keeps earning while you are away. Come back, collect your profit and invest it in the next floor.

GROW YOUR EMPIRE

Finish the first tower, buy a second hotel in a new part of the city and become the richest hotel tycoon in town.

FEATURES

  • Relaxing building gameplay with simple one-finger controls
  • Colorful 3D city made of toy bricks
  • 4 hero transformations and hero helpers
  • VIP guests arriving by helicopter
  • Dozens of room upgrades and decorations
  • Skins with bonuses and a 3D fitting room
  • Daily rewards, contracts, lucky wheel and achievements
  • Offline income
  • Play offline, no internet needed

Start with a single brick and build the hotel of your dreams!
