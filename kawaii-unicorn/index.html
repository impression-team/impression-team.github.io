About the game

New album "Winter Tale": 12 cozy pictures – snowman, penguin, reindeer, Christmas tree, gingerbread house, a unicorn in a snow globe and more!

Color cute unicorns, kittens, puppies and dozens of other adorable pictures in a cozy coloring book for everyone who loves kawaii art.

NEW ALBUMS

  • New album "Winter Tale": 12 cozy pictures – snowman, penguin on skates, polar bear cub with a gift, reindeer, Christmas tree, gingerbread house, kitten in a stocking, unicorn in a snow globe, hot cocoa, bunny on a sled, owlet in a hat and fox cub with a lantern
  • New album "Forest Friends": 10 cute pictures – squirrel, hedgehog, fox cub, bunny, owl, raccoon, bear cub, fawn, badger and beaver
  • Finish a picture to see a celebration and save your artwork to the gallery
  • Premium Forever: unlock everything with a one-time purchase

WHAT YOU CAN COLOR

  • Unicorns of every kind: mermaid, fairy, witch, sleeping, on the moon, with a cupcake or a balloon
  • Unicorn cats and cute animals: cat, dog, fox, panda, koala, bear, rabbit, lion, elephant, zebra
  • Sea friends: dolphin, turtle, jellyfish and swordfish
  • Holiday pictures for Halloween and Christmas
  • A blank canvas to draw your own picture from scratch

DRAWING TOOLS

  • Fill, pencil, crayon, brush, paint, roller and eraser
  • Glitter and rainbow gradients
  • Stamps to decorate your picture
  • Textures, shades of every color and your own custom palette
  • Zoom in for the smallest details

MADE FOR RELAXING

  • Calm music and no timers: color at your own pace
  • Works offline
  • Save finished pictures to your photo gallery

PREMIUM

Subscribe or buy Premium Forever to unlock all pictures, colors and textures, create custom palettes and remove ads. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them in your App Store account settings.
