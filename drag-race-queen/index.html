About the game

Version 4.0 is live: faster loads, smoother races, rebuilt for the newest iPhones. All 34 tracks, 10 bikes, and one very persistent police bike. Take the crown.

ONE BUTTON. ONE CROWN. ZERO BRAKES.

Drag Race Queen is a hold-and-go bike racer built for one thumb and pure speed. Press to open the throttle, release before the road eats you, and slam the needle to 200 before anyone else crosses the line.

HOLD TO GO FAST The whole game lives under one finger. Hold the throttle to build speed, let go a heartbeat before the chaos hits, then hold again the second the road opens up. Five seconds to learn. Level 30 will still humble you.

THE COPS ARE ON YOUR TAIL There's a police bike in every pack and it does NOT get tired. Pull ahead and it hunts you down. Clip it and it comes back angrier. Outrun the law — or get boxed in and watch the crown ride away without you.

WEAR THE CROWN Whoever's in front wears the crown, and everybody can see it. Steal it on the final straight. Lose it on one greasy patch of oil. Every single race is a street fight for first place.

34 TRACKS OF ROAD CHAOS Oil slicks. Launch ramps. Full vertical loops. Level crossings with trains that do not slow down for anyone. Broken asphalt, rolling barrels, and open water waiting for every rider who lands short. 34 hand-built tracks and 6 boss races where the whole thing kicks up a gear.

10 BIKES TO UNLOCK Street machines, custom builds and a few rides that have no business being on a public road. Win, unlock the garage, and pick the bike that fits your style. Every one of them has its own look, its own crew, its own attitude.

BUILT FOR QUICK RUNS Races are short, restarts are instant, and one more try is always one tap away. Perfect for a commute, a coffee queue, or the three hours you meant to spend doing literally anything else.

FEATURES

One-touch throttle — race with a single thumb

34 tracks and 6 boss races

10 unlockable bikes

A police pursuit in every single race

Ramps, loops, oil slicks, trains and traffic

Speed meter that climbs to 200

Instant restarts, zero waiting

Hold the throttle. Don't let go. Take the crown.
