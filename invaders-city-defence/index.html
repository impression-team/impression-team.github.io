About the game

New: Remove Ads option, coin packs in the shop and smoother waves. Hold the line and take the city back from the horde!

The city has fallen to the undead. Lead a special forces squad, clear the streets and hold every block you win back!

Invaders City Defence mixes fast top-down shooting with base-defense strategy and RPG progression. Aim, shoot, upgrade – and turn a ruined city into a fortress.

FIGHT THE HORDE

  • Control your hero, aim and battle waves of zombies in detailed city districts
  • Every wave hits harder – find the right mix of firepower and tactics

BUILD YOUR ARSENAL

  • Pistols, Uzis, shotguns and assault rifles
  • Upgrade damage, fire rate and range for devastating firepower

GROW STRONGER

  • Level up health, speed and attack range
  • Earn coins, unlock upgrades and push further into the infected zone

DEFEND YOUR TERRITORY

  • Hold captured zones with allies, traps and defensive upgrades
  • Destroy zombie nests and infected roots and watch the city come back to life

PLAY YOUR WAY

  • Short sessions or long runs – progress is always saved
  • Optional Remove Ads purchase and coin packs

Restore hope, save the survivors and become the hero the city needs. Download now and take the city back!
