Build a hospital empire

// The game

About the game

New: hire nurses and a driver, earn while you're away, collect daily rewards, grow your fleet and open new hospitals.

You have just taken over a struggling maternity hospital. Your job: turn it into the most profitable one in the city.

RUN THE OPERATION

Open new wards one by one and keep deliveries flowing. Every upgrade, from air conditioning to TVs to better furniture, raises a ward's throughput and your income.

HIRE YOUR STAFF

Put nurses on the conveyors and at the packing station, hire a driver for the limousine and watch the hospital run itself. Level up your staff to make them work faster.

MASTER THE LOGISTICS

Link up to 15 carts into a single convoy and steer it across the floor. Tight corners, full loads, no time to waste.

CLOSE THE DEAL

Fill the dispatch row and send the limousine. Grow your fleet from an ambulance to a Ferrari, a Lamborghini and a helicopter, and every run pays more. Watch out for rare VIP deliveries worth five times as much.

EARN WHILE YOU'RE AWAY

The hospital keeps earning when you close the game. Expand the vault to keep more, and come back every day for a reward with a 7-day streak.

SCALE UP

When every ward is maxed out, open a new hospital with a permanent income bonus and build an even bigger empire.

FEATURES

  • One-finger controls, easy to play on a short break
  • Staff automation, upgrades and a growing fleet
  • Offline earnings and daily rewards
  • Auto-save: continue exactly where you stopped
  • Plays offline
  • No energy timers

Take over the hospital and survive the Labor Rush.
