Ocean diving adventure game

iPhone · iPad Android
// The game

About the game

Five oceans, one tank of air. Dive for pearls, pirate gold and lost idols, dodge the sharks and make it back to the boat before your gauge hits zero!

One tank of air. A whole ocean of treasure. How deep will you dare to go?

Tap DIVE, slip off the boat and steer your scuba diver through reefs, shipwrecks and sunken ruins. Fill your loot bag with pearls and pirate gold – then get back to the surface before the air runs out.

Sea Diver is a scuba diving game about "just one more dive". Every trip earns coins, every coin buys better gear, and better gear takes you deeper.

DIVE AND EXPLORE

  • Huge, hand-crafted underwater worlds – each location is 50 to 150 screens of seabed, coastal cliffs, coral reefs, kelp forests, caves and wrecks
  • Smooth joystick controls with a real underwater feel: build up speed, glide, and fight the drag
  • Currents that sweep you sideways or lift you up, and darkness that grows the deeper you go – pack a flashlight

COLLECT TREASURE

  • Open pearl oysters for white, pink, black and rare golden pearls
  • Find precious coral, starfish, amphorae, gold coins, gems, golden idols, crowns and messages in bottles
  • Hold to crack open pirate treasure chests
  • Sell your haul and watch the coins pile up

WATCH YOUR AIR

  • Your air gauge never stops draining. When it turns red, head for the boat
  • Run out and your diver is lost – along with everything in the bag
  • Risk it for one more chest, or play it safe? Your call, every single dive

DANGER BELOW

  • Reef sharks, great whites and Greenland sharks patrol the deep
  • Barracudas, jellyfish, moray eels, pufferfish, leopard seals, and anglerfish lurking in the dark
  • Fight back with a harpoon gun and nets, or keep them away with shark repellent
  • Electric eels that zap you – and friendly turtles and dolphins to hitch a ride on

UPGRADE YOUR GEAR

  • A bigger air tank, faster fins, a wetsuit for deeper dives and better protection
  • Harpoon gun, net launcher, flashlight, bigger loot bag, spare air
  • Upgrade the boat: sonar to find treasure, a larger cargo hold, a stronger engine
  • Trade your dinghy for a trawler, a speedboat, a yacht – and finally a golden yacht

FIVE OCEANS TO CONQUER

  • Indian Ocean – warm tropical reefs, perfect for your first dives
  • Atlantic Ocean – pirate shipwrecks and sunken ruins
  • Pacific Ocean – volcanic vents and the deepest trench on the planet
  • Southern Ocean – kelp forests, icebergs and penguins
  • Arctic Ocean – dive under the pack ice

PLAY YOUR WAY

  • Works offline – no Wi-Fi and no account needed
  • Short dives for a coffee break, long expeditions for the couch
  • Available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Italian and Russian

Sea Diver is free to download and play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases (Starter pack, Remove ads, coin packs, the Treasure hunter license and the Golden yacht). Rewarded ads are always your choice: emergency air to revive once per dive, x2 coins on the results screen, a free gift every 4 hours, or +25% air before a dive.

Check your gauge, grab your fins and dive in. The ocean is not going to loot itself.
