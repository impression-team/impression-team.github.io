About the game

Swap, match and blast your way through 100+ sweet levels! Collect daily rewards, spin for bonuses and help the cheerful bee explore a colorful candy meadow.

Buzz into Bee Rush, a sweet match 3 puzzle game full of candy, bright colors and a cheerful little bee who can't wait to explore a blooming meadow with you!

Swap and match 3 candies of the same color, create juicy combos and blast through 100+ levels. Easy to learn and fun to master, Bee Rush is the perfect relaxing puzzle game to play anytime.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Swap neighboring candies to match 3 or more of the same color
  • Match 4 candies to make a Striped Candy that clears a whole row
  • Match 5 in an L or T shape to make a Package Candy that blasts everything around it
  • Match 5 in a row to make a Multicolor Bomb that removes every candy of one color
  • Combine special candies for huge chain reactions

SWEET CHALLENGES

  • Reach the target score before you run out of moves
  • Spread jelly across the whole board
  • Bring the ingredients down to the bottom
  • Collect the required items
  • Beat the clock in timed levels
  • Break chocolate, sugar blocks, cages and spirals, and defuse time bombs

FEATURES

  • 100+ fun match-3 levels on a colorful adventure map
  • Helpful boosters: extra moves, extra time, Choco Spiral and more
  • Daily rewards and a lucky Bonus Spin
  • A cute bee hero and bright, relaxing graphics
  • Short levels, perfect for a quick puzzle break
  • Any purchase removes ads

Bee Rush is free to download and play. Some in-game items can be purchased for real money. The game contains ads.
