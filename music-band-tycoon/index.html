About the game

NEW: daily rewards, daily quests and VIP fans who pay 5x! Raise ticket prices, double your income and come back to cash your band earned while you were away.

From a street corner to a sold-out arena — build the biggest band in the world!

You started with one guitar, an empty hat and zero fans. Play, gather a crowd, hire musicians and turn your garage band into a money-printing music empire. Tap once, then watch the cash pile up — even while you're offline!

GATHER THE CROWD

Busk on the street and fans will literally follow you. The louder your sound, the bigger the crowd behind you. Lead them to your stage and fill every seat.

BUILD YOUR DREAM BAND

Unlock instrument after instrument: acoustic guitar, violin, synth, sax, piano, drums, backing choir, bass and screaming rock guitar. Every new musician means a better show and more money.

UNLOCK BIGGER VENUES

Start on a street corner, move up to a smoky jazz club, finish on a massive rock arena. Every venue you open multiplies your income.

CASH IN ON THE CROWD

Open ice cream, popcorn and drink stands around your stage. Hungry fans mean extra profit on every single concert.

IDLE INCOME 24/7

Your band keeps playing while you sleep. Come back to a pile of cash and spend it on the next upgrade.

DRESS THE STAR

Collect hats and costumes and walk on stage in style.

  • Free to play
  • One-hand controls, easy to learn
  • Works offline — no Wi-Fi needed
  • Short sessions, perfect for a coffee break

Ready to headline? Grab your guitar and start playing!
