About the game

Ram the horde in your armored jeep, burn out the nests, hold the base when night falls and craft the vaccine that turns zombies back into people!

Eight very different heroes, one armored jeep with a plow and the only cure for the outbreak. Ram through the infected streets, rescue the survivors and turn the zombies back into people!

Crush Crew: Zombie Survival is an action-packed zombie shooter with car crashing, squad battles, base defense and boss fights. Smash through the undead horde in an armored jeep, jump out with your heroes to clear the zones on foot, and bring the survivors home before night falls.

DRIVE THROUGH THE HORDE

  • Plow through crowds of zombies in your armored off-road jeep
  • Knock over cars, barricades, barrels and street lamps - the city reacts to every crash
  • Mount a machine gun or a rocket pod on the roof, upgrade armor, engine and spiked wheels

CLEAR THE ZONES WITH YOUR CREW

  • Lead a squad of unique heroes: assault riflemen, snipers, a heavy gunner, an engineer and a medic
  • Your heroes aim and shoot automatically - you choose where to go and what to hit
  • Burn out infected nests, find sleeping zombies and push through toxic districts

DEFEND THE BASE AT NIGHT

  • When the sun goes down, the dead come for your base - wave after wave
  • Build machine-gun posts on the watchtowers, upgrade the gates and switch on the searchlights
  • Hold the line until dawn, then repair the gates before the next night

DEFEAT THE BOSSES

  • Every district hides a boss lair: a hospital basement, a bank vault, a prison cell block, a red barn and more
  • Dodge slams and acid, focus fire and take the zombie DNA they carry

RESCUE SURVIVORS

  • Find people calling for help, escort them to your jeep and drive them home
  • Rescued survivors unlock new crew members and grow your base

MAKE THE VACCINE - SAVE THE CITY

  • Collect zombie DNA from the bosses and bring it to the doctor's tent
  • Eight samples make the vaccine: give your medic the antidote pistol and turn zombies back into people!

A WHOLE CITY TO EXPLORE

  • Sea port, airport, military base, supermarket, school, prison, farm, rail depot and more
  • Day and night cycle: fight in the sun, then switch on flashlights and headlights after dark
  • Open map with goals, danger levels and districts to clear in any order

FEATURES

  • Fast, satisfying car crashing and auto-shooting action
  • Squad building with hero upgrades, ranks and gear
  • Base building and night siege defense
  • Boss fights in unique lairs
  • Play offline - no internet needed
  • Crew Pass season with free and premium rewards

Ready to crush the horde? Download Crush Crew: Zombie Survival and save the city!
