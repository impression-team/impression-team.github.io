About the game

Aim, shoot and pop your way through colorful levels! Smash blockers with powerful boosters and rescue the fish in this relaxing bubble shooter.

Bubble Rush Shooter is a colorful and relaxing bubble shooter puzzle game – easy to learn, hard to put down! Join the cheerful fox, aim carefully and match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them and clear the board.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag to aim, release to shoot
  • Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them
  • Bounce shots off the walls to reach tricky spots
  • Drop whole clusters at once for a huge score
  • Swap your next bubble to plan the perfect shot
  • Complete the level goal before you run out of bubbles

GAME FEATURES

  • Hand-crafted levels on a beautiful adventure map
  • Varied goals: clear bubbles, collect falling leaves and rescue trapped fish
  • Powerful boosters: Bomb, Color Bomb, Line Bomb and Rainbow Bubble
  • Tricky blockers to beat: stone, ice, iron, sticky, cloud and void bubbles
  • Fill the Energy Orb to unleash a super-powered Energy Bubble
  • Earn up to 3 stars on every level and beat your high score
  • Bright graphics, juicy pop effects and relaxing music
  • Short levels – perfect for a quick break anytime

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

Bubble Rush Shooter mixes classic bubble pop gameplay with fresh match 3 puzzle challenges. Plan every shot, pick the right booster at the right moment and enjoy satisfying chain reactions that clear the screen!

Bubble Rush Shooter is free to play. Optional in-app purchases are available. The game contains ads.

Ready to pop? Download Bubble Rush Shooter now and start your bubble adventure!
