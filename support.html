Support

Need help with one of our games? Check the answers below or write to us — we read every message and usually reply within 1–2 business days.

Crush Crew: Zombie Survival

Crush Crew: Zombie Survival

iOS Android

Drive an armored jeep through the zombie horde, clear infested districts with your crew, defend the base at night and collect zombie DNA to craft the vaccine that saves the city.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings (the gear button in the top-right corner) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. Gems and other consumables are added to your game when bought and cannot be restored; the Starter Pack, No Ads, the Crew Pass and VIP are restored automatically.

I bought something but did not get it.

Close the game completely and open it again while connected to the internet — pending purchases are delivered on the next launch. If it still does not appear, use Restore Purchases in Settings. Still missing? Write to us with the date of the purchase and the receipt from Apple or Google.

How do I remove ads?

The No Ads pack in the Shop removes all interstitial ads forever. Rewarded videos (for revives and bonuses) stay optional — you only watch them when you choose to.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game or clearing its data removes the save, so please do not delete the app if you want to keep your crew, base and city progress. Purchases tied to your store account can be restored after reinstalling.

The game runs slowly or my phone gets hot.

Close other apps, let the phone cool down and keep it charged — low battery and heat make the system slow the game down. Night battles with many lights and big sieges are the most demanding moments. If the problem persists, tell us your device model.

How do I make the vaccine?

Defeat the bosses of the districts and bring their zombie DNA to the Doctor's Tent at your base. Eight DNA samples make the vaccine — then give Doc the antidote pistol in Heroes and turn zombies back into people.

I found a bug or have an idea.

We would love to hear it! Write to us with your device model, the game version (Settings screen) and what happened. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot.
