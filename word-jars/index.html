// The game

About the game

Sort letters, solve cozy themed puzzles, and grow your pantry collection. No timers, no losing — play at your own pace.

Word Jars is a cozy word puzzle for people who love crosswords and quiet brain games.

Letter balls are mixed up in glass jars. Tap a jar to lift the top ball, tap another jar to drop it in. Sort the balls until every jar spells a word on the level's theme, reading bottom to top. A finished jar gets a screw-on lid, and the word goes into your notebook.

60 THEMES

Garden, Kitchen, Birds, Fairy Tales, Space, Music, Seaside and many more. Every theme gives several levels, and words never repeat within a theme.

NO TIMER, NO LOSING

Think as long as you like. There is always a way to finish the level.

A NOTEBOOK THAT HELPS

The notebook shows how long each word is and the first letter of the first word. Hidden "?" balls reveal their letter when they reach the top of a jar.

HANDY BOOSTERS

Hint opens a letter in the notebook, +1 Jar adds an empty jar, Undo takes back five moves, Reveal ? shows hidden letters.

PANTRY

Every theme has its own shelf in the pantry. Finished levels stand there as sealed jars: tap a jar to see its words. The daily word lives in the pantry too: a new short puzzle every day. Keep your streak going!

CALM AND CLEAR

Large letters, warm colors and a wooden shelf with glass jars. Plays offline.

The game is free with ads between levels and optional rewarded ads for boosters. You can remove ads and buy booster packs in the shop.
