About the game

All aboard! Lay the rails in front of a rolling steam engine, cross rivers, deserts and lava fields, and pull into the station on time.

All aboard! Your steam locomotive is already rolling – and there are no rails ahead. Build the track piece by piece before the train gets there and keep it from going off the rails!

Trains and Rails is a relaxing yet thrilling track-building puzzle in a cosy steampunk style. Drag straight rails, curves and switches from the tray, drop them onto the map and watch them snap into place right in front of your brass-and-copper engine.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag a rail piece from the tray – it snaps into place when you get close
  • Keep the track going: the train never stops and follows every rail you lay
  • Don't need a piece? Toss it into the bin and grab a new one
  • Tap switches to change the route, and tap wild animals to scare them off the tracks with the whistle
  • Short on time? Hold the steam brake. Feeling brave? Hit full steam ahead!

A JOURNEY ACROSS FIVE REGIONS

Travel from the Green Plains through the Copper Desert, the Iron Mountains and the Frost Pass all the way to the fiery Ember Lands. 30 routes and 90 stars are waiting for you.

OBSTACLES ON EVERY ROUTE

Rivers with bridges, level crossings with steam cars, mountain tunnels, lakes, rocks, forests and wild animals – every route needs a new plan.

PASSENGERS AND COINS

Pick up passengers at wayside halts, collect coins along the way and pull into the grand station at the end of each route.

YOUR OWN STEAM TRAIN

Upgrade the boiler, steam brakes, carriages, repair crew and coin magnet in the depot. Every upgrade makes your train stronger and changes how it looks! Unlock new liveries, from Copper Comet to Emerald Express.

FEATURES

  • Hand-crafted steampunk look with an animated steam locomotive: turning wheels, moving rods, smoke and steam
  • Easy to learn, fun to master track-building puzzles
  • 30 routes in 5 unique regions, from sunny plains to snowstorms and lava
  • Train upgrades and liveries
  • Cheerful steampunk soundtrack
  • Short sessions – perfect for a quick ride

Build the track. Save the train. Enjoy the ride!

The game is free to play and contains ads and optional in-app purchases.
